# iOS touch controls

Touch controls and their icons are included with the app and are inherited from Carlox33's Android port. They switch layouts for menus, on-foot play and driving.

Use the in-game touch customization menu to adjust button placement and size. The generated configuration is stored in:

```text
Documents/touch_controls/Simpsons_touch_controls_configuration.txt
```

In Files, `Documents` is the **Simpsons Hit & Run** folder under **On My iPhone / On My iPad**. Touch settings belong outside `game`. Close the app and back up the file before editing it manually, then reopen the app to apply changes.

For external gamepads see [controller controls](CONTROLLER_CONTROLS.md). The [original multilingual touch guide](docs/android-history/TOUCH_CONTROLS.md) is preserved for attribution and background; its Android paths and version announcements describe the upstream project.
